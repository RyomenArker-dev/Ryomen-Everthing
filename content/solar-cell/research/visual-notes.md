# Visual notes (from general knowledge of typical modules; no photos embedded)

- Mono PERC/TOPCon cells look deep blue-black with a faint violet sheen at angles; poly cells are speckled blue (we show mono, the mainstream).
- Busbars: thin bright silver lines across the cell; modern cells have 9–16 very thin "multi-busbar" wires — we show 5 for clarity (illustrative).
- Fingers: hair-thin silver lines perpendicular to busbars, too dense to see individually from more than ~1 m.
- Frame: matte anodised aluminium, silver or black; backsheet white.
