## Sources to cite if asked
Check these before publishing.
- Freeth, T. et al. (2006). *Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism.* Nature 444. This is the X-ray CT study.
- Freeth, T. et al. (2021). *A Model of the Cosmos in the ancient Greek Antikythera Mechanism.* Scientific Reports 11. This covers the planetary display, reconstructed from inscriptions.
- National Archaeological Museum, Athens, which displays the fragments.

Accuracy notes:
- The wreck was found by sponge divers in 1900, and objects were recovered in 1900–1901.
- The device is usually dated to the 2nd–1st century BC.
- The five planets are inferred mainly from inscriptions read in the CT scans, because most of the planetary gearing is lost.
