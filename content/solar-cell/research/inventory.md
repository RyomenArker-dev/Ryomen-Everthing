# Inventory — what the camera sees at each level

1. **Rooftop / panel**: framed module (aluminium frame, dark glass), 6 × 12 grid of blue-black cells (72-cell layout [S3]), thin white gaps, silver busbar ribbons running across cells. Sunlight from one side.
2. **One cell (182 mm square [S7])**: blue SiNx surface with fine pyramid texture (sheen), dense silver fingers (~50 µm, a few mm apart), 4–5 wider busbars with ribbons.
3. **Edge cross-section (illustrative, not to scale)**: glass 3.2 mm → EVA → SiNx ARC (thin blue film) → n-type emitter (<1 µm) → p-type base (~150 µm) → aluminium rear contact (~30 µm) → EVA → backsheet.
4. **Junction close-up (illustrative)**: depletion region at n|p boundary; fixed charges (+ on n side, − on p side); field arrow n→p.
5. **Photon event**: photon (≥1.12 eV, < 1107 nm) enters through glass/ARC, absorbed in silicon → electron (−) + hole (+).
6. **Separation**: electron drifts to n side → finger; hole drifts to p side → rear contact.
7. **Circuit**: wire from busbar ribbon → load (bulb lights) → back to rear contact; electrons flow continuously.
