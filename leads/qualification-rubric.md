# Lead qualification rubric

Score each criterion 0–5 **with a written reason based on observed, sourced evidence**.
No evidence → score 0 and note `UNKNOWN`. Total /20.

| Criterion | 0 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Need** — visible gap our services fix | none seen | some gaps (e.g. dated site, inactive socials) | clear, important gap (no website, broken mobile layout, no GBP) |
| **Fit** — matches our ideal client (`agency/positioning.md`) | outside niche/location | partly | exact match |
| **Ability to pay (signals)** — public signals only | none | established business (years trading, multiple locations, staff) | strong signals (ad activity, premium positioning) |
| **Reachability** — published business contact channel | none | generic form only | business email/phone published |

| Total | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 15–20 | Qualified — high | Draft outreach (approval needed) |
| 10–14 | Qualified — medium | Nurture / optional outreach |
| 0–9 | Not qualified | Archive with reason |

Never infer revenue, owner identity or personal details. Never mark a lead "qualified" on guesses.
