# Type I to Type VII Civilisations

## intro {chapter="The idea"}
How advanced could a civilisation become? [#q] In 1964, the Soviet astronomer *Nikolai Kardashev* suggested a simple measure: [#energy] how much *energy* it can use.

## sagan
Carl Sagan later made it a sliding scale. [#steps] Each type uses about *ten billion* times more power than the one before.

## type1 {chapter="Kardashev's three types"}
A *Type One* civilisation uses power on the scale of its whole *planet*. [#watts] On Sagan's scale, that's ten to the sixteen watts.

## humans
We're not there yet. [#2021] In 2021, humanity used about nineteen terawatts. [#rating] That makes us roughly *Type zero point seven three*.

## type2
A *Type Two* civilisation harnesses its entire *star*. [#dyson] One idea is a swarm of collectors around it, known as a *Dyson sphere*.

## type3
A *Type Three* civilisation commands a whole *galaxy*: [#stars] the light of billions of stars.

## beyond {chapter="Types IV to VII"}
That's where Kardashev stopped. [#spec] Everything above Type Three is *speculation* by later thinkers, and they don't agree.

## type4
*Type Four* would reach beyond its galaxy. [#kaku] Physicist Michio Kaku imagined it drawing energy from between the galaxies. [#popular] Popular versions say the whole *universe*.

## type5
*Type Five*, in popular versions, spans many universes: [#multi] a *multiverse*. [#galantai] Futurist Zoltán Galántai's scale calls this stage technically immortal.

## type67 {transition=fade}
*Types Six and Seven* come mostly from internet videos and fiction. [#six] Six masters *space and time*. [#seven] Seven is god-like, able to create universes. [#nopaper] No scientific paper defines them.

## outro {transition=fade}
So on the real scale, we're still climbing towards *Type One*. [#rest] Everything beyond that is imagination, for now.
