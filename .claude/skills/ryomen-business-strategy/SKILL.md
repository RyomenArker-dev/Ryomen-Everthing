# Ryomen — Business Strategy

## Inputs to collect (ask if missing; never assume)
- Current services and what the owner can actually deliver alone
- Target market (location, industries, business size), currency
- Current or desired income target and weekly hours available
- Existing clients/portfolio (only what the owner tells you)

## Steps
1. Read `agency/services.md`, `agency/pricing.md`, `agency/positioning.md`.
2. Identify the decision being made (new package? price change? niche?). State it in one line.
3. Produce 2–3 options. For each: who it's for, deliverables, scope limits, delivery time,
   pricing *model* (fixed, tiered, retainer, hourly) and price **range only if sourced**;
   otherwise `TODO: owner to set`. Show margin logic (hours × target hourly rate) using the
   owner's numbers.
4. Recommend one option and explain trade-offs in plain language.
5. After owner confirmation, update the relevant `agency/*.md` file. Note the change in a
   "Change log" section at the bottom with the date.

## For deeper work, load
`pricing` (value metrics, tiers), `offers` (guarantees, bonuses), `product-marketing`
(positioning/ICP), `marketing-plan` (90-day plan), `marketing-council` (multiple perspectives).

## Output rules
- Market data must cite a source URL + date, or be labelled `UNVERIFIED`.
- Never claim results, client counts or credentials the owner has not given you.
